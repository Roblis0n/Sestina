# Code of Conduct

Sestina welcomes respectful, evidence-minded collaboration. Participation in
repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces requires the following standard.

## Expected behavior

- Address ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Be precise about what is observed, inferred, unknown, or unproven.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, and research confidentiality.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty or correction.
- Make room for different languages, backgrounds, access needs, and levels of
  experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
private research or credentials, impersonation, deliberate misinformation,
and sustained disruption are not accepted. Do not pressure another person to
disclose private data or hidden reasoning.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations,
reject changes, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community and repository. If a report contains sensitive information, use a
private GitHub Security Advisory as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead of posting it publicly. Reports will be reviewed with attention to the
privacy and safety of everyone involved.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project.
